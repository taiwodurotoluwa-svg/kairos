    The dispute lab

    When the evidence
    doesn’t agree.

    Contradictions aren’t dead ends. They’re invitations to look closer. Put two claims side by side and investigate the gap.

    Study A / claim 01—A
    “The intervention significantly improved long-term recall.”
    Controlled sample · n=240 2024 ↗
    VS.
    Study B / claim 02—B
    “No reliable improvement was observed after four weeks.”
    Replication · n=612 2025 ↗
    Referee notes / preview

    Not so fast.

    These claims may not be direct opposites. The studies could differ in follow-up duration, participant profile, or how “improvement” was measured. A contradiction is a signal to inspect the methods—not a verdict.
